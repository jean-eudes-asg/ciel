Asks before anything runs

Nothing runs without your OK.

When an agent wants to run a command or change a file, the island slides down with Deny and Allow. Ciel never says yes on his own, and if he isn't running, your agent simply asks in its terminal as usual.

Dictation and Notetaker

Hold Right Ctrl and talk.

Speak anywhere, let go, and your words are typed where you were. The Notetaker records a call and hands you the transcript, person by person, with a summary and the to-dos. The speech is recognised offline, on your own CPU.

« OK Ciel »

Ask out loud. Nothing leaves your computer.

Ask for your pills, your music, what you spent this month, or to open an app. Ciel answers with Claude and your own key, or with a model on your own machine through Ollama, such as qwen3.5:9b. Then nothing leaves your computer.

Finance pill

Where the money went, at a glance.

This month against your budget, the top categories and the latest entries. Add an expense in a few clicks, or just tell Ciel “I spent 12 euros on lunch”. It all stays in two plain files, in a folder you choose.

Open source

Read the code, fork it, make it yours. Ciel is built in the open with Tauri, Rust and TypeScript, and every bit of him (the cloud, the icon, the sounds, the skies) is drawn or synthesised in code.

Install

Three steps, then he's on duty.

  1. Download

    Get the installer, Ciel-Windows-setup.exe, from the latest release. Windows 10 and 11.

    Latest release

  2. Run it anyway

    The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may stop it. Click More info, then Run anyway.

    Windows protected your PC More info Run anyway

  3. Connect your agents

    Ciel appears at the top of your screen. Open Settings → Claude Code → Install hooks… (and the page of each other agent you use): you see the exact change and a dated backup is kept before anything is written.

On Linux

Privacy

What happens on your computer stays there.

  • No telemetry, no account

    Ciel only talks to the services you set up yourself. Its log stays on your machine.

  • Keys in your keychain

    API keys live in Windows Credential Manager or the Linux Secret Service, never in a file.

  • Your voice stays home

    Dictation and the Notetaker transcribe on your CPU. Only text goes to the AI you choose.

  • Local models

    Point « OK Ciel », the chat and the call summaries at Ollama or LM Studio, and nothing leaves your computer.
